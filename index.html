Health Information

🩸 Blood Pressure

Blood pressure measures the force of blood against your artery walls. Consistently abnormal readings should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

🍬 Blood Sugar

Blood glucose levels can vary depending on meals, medications and other factors. Repeated abnormal readings require professional evaluation.

❤️ Pulse

Pulse rate is the number of heartbeats per minute. A person's normal resting pulse can vary depending on age, fitness and other factors.

🌡️ Temperature

An elevated temperature may occur with infection or other conditions. Consider other symptoms when deciding whether to seek medical attention.
